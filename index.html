Tax time

rough estimate

Uses gross pay and income tax from the pay stub details on each paycheque. Doesn't count RRSP or FHSA deductions, which lower what you owe, or other income. Roommate rent on a cost-sharing basis usually isn't taxable, but check CRA's rules. Not tax advice.

Sync & backup

Your data lives only on this phone. To share with your partner's phone: tap Send backup, send the file (WhatsApp, email, Drive), and on the other phone tap Merge a backup. Merging keeps the newest version of everything and never duplicates. Do it both ways to stay in step.
